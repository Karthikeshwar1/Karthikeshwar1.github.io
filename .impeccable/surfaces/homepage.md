## Direction contract

THESIS: A stark, ultra-minimal single-focus exhibition that refuses the category-default grid of mixed content, paired with a structured, physical-feeling archive for deep exploration.

OWN-WORLD: "The Quiet Gallery." High-contrast monochrome, pure white paper-like ground, and classic Calendas Plus serif type. It feels like an enduring, classic manuscript rather than a tech portfolio.

STORY: The visitor arrives to immerse immediately in a single, curated piece of work (the plinth), feeling the weight of the words or art, before intentionally navigating to the deeper archive.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full viewport dedicated to one perfectly centered featured piece—a poem snippet or art thumbnail—with a subtle horizontal navigation menu below. No clutter, no scroll-to-discover on the home view.

FORM: The Focused Plinth (Index 7) for the homepage, joined with The Split Canvas (Index 4) for the project archive. Seed key: b1b904f5.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
